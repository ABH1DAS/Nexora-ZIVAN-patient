# 🩺 ZIVAN

### *Live Healthy. Stay Ready.*

> **An AI-powered health, wellbeing and emergency support ecosystem.**

---

## ✨ About ZIVAN

**ZIVAN** brings together daily health tracking, mental wellbeing, healthy habits and emergency assistance into one connected platform.

Instead of using multiple disconnected applications, ZIVAN helps users:

**Track → Analyze → Improve → Protect → Respond**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🏠 Health Dashboard
- Heart rate monitoring
- Steps and activity tracking
- Sleep insights
- Hydration tracking
- Personal wellbeing score

### 🧠 Mental Wellbeing
- Daily mood check-ins
- Stress tracking
- Wellness suggestions
- Breathing and relaxation activities

### 💧 Healthy Habits
- Water goals
- Sleep goals
- Exercise tracking
- Daily progress and streaks

### 🤖 AI Health Assistant
- General health and wellbeing guidance
- Personalized insights based on user activity
- Health trend understanding

> ⚠️ ZIVAN does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services.
